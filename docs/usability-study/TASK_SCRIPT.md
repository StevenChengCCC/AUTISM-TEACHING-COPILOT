# Neutral task script

1. “Using this synthetic profile and request, prepare a package you would be willing to review for classroom use.”
2. “Find the print choice intended for the teacher’s desk, choose the requested paper and text size, and download it.”
3. “Tell me what you would check before using the printout. I am evaluating the product, not your teaching decisions.”
4. “Start a session using the downloaded artifact.”
5. “Record the five observations on the case card. One is invalid and one uses AAC.”
6. “Reload the page, then continue from where you left off.”
7. “Complete the closeout from the provided observations.”
8. “Review progress and next-session guidance. Tell me what you believe it says and what remains uncertain.”
9. “Compare this workflow with how you currently prepare, record, and close out a similar lesson.”

If assistance is requested, ask: “What were you expecting to find?” Then give the smallest necessary hint and record it.
