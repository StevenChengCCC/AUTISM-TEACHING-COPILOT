# Facilitator guide

## Before the session

- Confirm consent language has completed legal/privacy review.
- Use a reset development or authorized non-production environment with synthetic data only.
- Assign a pseudonym such as P-001. Do not record the participant’s name in product telemetry.
- Prepare Letter and A4 paper, color and low-ink printer options, and a phone/tablet plus desktop browser.
- Verify study measurement shows Disabled before discussing opt-in.
- Explain that the product is being evaluated, not the teacher.

## During the session

Read task prompts exactly. Avoid hints unless the participant asks; record every assistance request. Record system delay separately from human task time. Never enter real learner information. Stop for any privacy, safety, or distress concern.

## After the session

Collect neutral ratings, export only consented privacy-safe events, reconcile them with the observation sheet, offer participant deletion, and record issues before reviewing thresholds.
