# Participant information and consent — draft for legal/privacy review

This is research-planning language, not legal advice. It must be reviewed by the responsible organization before use.

You are invited to evaluate a teaching-support prototype using synthetic learner cases. The session lasts about 60 minutes. We will ask you to prepare and print a lesson kit, record synthetic trials, recover after a reload, close a session, and interpret progress and next-session guidance.

With your explicit permission, the local study tool records only task codes, timestamps, durations, interaction counts, outcome/error codes, ratings, a study pseudonym, and a synthetic case ID. It does not record learner or lesson content, free text, documents, prompts, PDF contents, keystrokes, session replay, direct identifiers, or full URLs.

Participation is voluntary. You may skip a question or stop at any time without penalty. You may ask for your study events to be exported or deleted. Do not enter information about a real learner, family, colleague, or school.
