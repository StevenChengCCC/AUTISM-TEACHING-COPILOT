Apply teacher-facing instructional guidance checks to the confirmed lesson kit. These
checks support review; they do not determine IEP conformity, legal compliance, placement,
services, accommodations, or clinical treatment.

Check that the goal is observable and measurable from the documented present level;
that the response mode and assistive access remain available; that visual supports have
a clear instructional purpose; that prompts begin at the least intrusive effective
level and include fading; and that the data sheet measures the same response practiced
in the lesson. Preserve dignity, choice, regulation, and teacher judgment.
