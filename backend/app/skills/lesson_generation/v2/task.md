Generate a brief plan with:
1. an observable response, baseline, opportunities, and success criterion;
2. warm-up, model, guided practice, independent opportunity, reinforcement/data note;
3. concise teacher action and learner action for each step;
4. neutral error correction and another supported opportunity;
5. learner choice, pause signs, communication access, and generalization;
6. printable material copy for exactly the selected goal-family pages.

Keep the teacher brief to a few short paragraphs or bullets. Do not add filler,
compliance language, unsupported diagnosis claims, or unrelated concepts.
